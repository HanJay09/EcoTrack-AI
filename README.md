# 🌱 EcoTrack AI

EcoTrack AI is a **generative AI–powered sustainability assistant** built using **AWS PartyRock**.  
The app helps users track, understand, and reduce their **personal carbon footprint** by analyzing daily activities and generating actionable insights.

---

## 🚩 Problem Statement
Many people want to live more sustainably but lack visibility into how their daily activities contribute to carbon emissions.  
Carbon footprints from transportation, food, electricity, and consumer purchases are difficult to measure and understand without technical tools.

---

## 💡 Our Solution
EcoTrack AI allows users to:
- Input daily activities (transport, food, electricity, purchases)
- Estimate carbon emissions using AI-powered reasoning
- Receive personalized recommendations to reduce emissions
- Generate weekly and monthly sustainability progress summaries
- Interact with an AI chatbot for sustainability education

The app is designed to be **simple, friendly, and beginner-friendly**, making sustainability accessible to everyone.
